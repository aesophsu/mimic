# Figure 3 Caption

Figure 3. Performance evaluation, calibration, and robustness of the predictive models.
a) Receiver operating characteristic (ROC) curves of the development algorithms in the internal cohort.
b) Comparison of ROC curves between the full-feature and parsimonious models in the external cohort.
c) and d) Calibration plots for the internal and external cohorts, respectively; the histograms represent the distribution of predicted risks.
e) Sensitivity analysis showing the ROC curves for the non-renal failure subgroup compared with the overall population, demonstrating model robustness.
f) Summary of AUC values with 95% confidence intervals across different cohorts and clinical subgroups.
